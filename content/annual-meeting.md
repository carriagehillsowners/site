# Announcements From The Board

Hello, neighbors of Carriage Hills! We hope that you have thoroughly enjoyed your summer!

## Owner Contact Information

The Board asks that you PLEASE ensure you maintain a **CURRENT** _owner name, mailing address, e-mail address, and phone number_, 
with the Secretary, as well as directing any future buyer of your home to provide the same information.

Additionally, the Board kindly requests that you ALSO provide the phone number and email of any tenant you may be renting 
your home to. This information helps ensure that all important communications reach you and/or your tenant, especially in the 
event of any emergency situation occurring.

## Property Upkeep

The Board strives to levy minimal enforcement action against homes in violation of the [Covenants & Bylaws](/documents#covenants--bylaws).

To achieve this requires Owners ensuring that minimum standards of property maintenance are upheld. The entire community benefits from 
higher property values and a more aesthetically pleasing neighborhood when properties are properly upkept. The Board recommends using a
commercial lawn care service if you are struggling to find time to keep up with lawn care. Performing weed treatment of your yard can
help extend the time necessary between cuts.

Recommendations for such services can be found on the [resources](/resources) page. If you should decide to rent your home the Board 
reminds you that you could be held liable for any issues arising from your tenant(s), such as failing to upkeep your property. To
avoid this, the Board suggests building the cost of commercial lawn care service into rent charges.

Additionally, if you may be in need of one-time help then Scouts may be able to help you out, to build service hours! Reach out
to the Board President for details.

## Children At Play

Speeding has been a persistent problem in the neighborhood. We ask that all residents be mindful of the uptick in children and
residents walking in the neighborhood in recent years. As such, the Board kindly requests your cooperation in watching your
speed within the neighborhood. As well, if residents can please try to keep vehicles off the street, or at least not parked 
opposite a vehicle on the other side of the street, it would help immensely with ensuring the safety of bicycles and pedestrians
within the neighborhood.

## Neighborhood Watch & Looking Out For Your Neighbors

The Board is looking to open dialog with the Columbia County Sheriff's Office to re-establish the Neighborhood Watch program 
in our neighborhood. If you might be interested in being a part of the dialog, or potentially receiving training, then please 
reach out the Secretary at <carriagehills.mail@gmail.com> with an email subject of "Neighborhood Watch."

In the interim we ask that you lookout for your neighbors and notify CCSO if you should observe anything suspicious taking 
place within the neighborhood. Additional, we would like to remind you that you can notify CCSO prior to any extended time
out of town, and they may be able to watch over your property for you.

## Additional Internet Providers

Owners have previously expressed interest in gaining more diversity in Internet providers within the neighborhood. Columbia
County maintains it's own fiber ring through C3BU, with West Carolina Fiber servicing last mile residential fiber service to
the home. In order for a neighborhood to be built out by West Carolina there must be a service interest expressed by a 
certain percentage of a neighborhood's households. Jones Ferry, off Furys Ferry Rd, is an example of a neighborhood in the 
area that has been built out with West Carolina Fiber service. More providers often translates to lower prices offered
by each provider, as well as more choices if the incumbent providers are not providing you stable service. To register interest
go [here](https://www.gigupgreenwood.com).
