Welcome to the official website of the *Carriage Hills Home Owners Association*! This site serves as a resource to keep all Owners within the Association informed on Association happenings, as well as a reference for covenants and bylaws of the Association.

---

### About Us
Located in Martinez, Georgia, just across from the River Island Community, and centrally located next to top-rated [Stallings Island Middle School](https://stallingsislandms.ccboe.net/), Carriage Hills offers peaceful living in a friendly and welcoming neighborhood!
